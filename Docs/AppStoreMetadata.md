## App description

Personal style begins with the details you notice. Tutu brings curated fashion videos, outfit ideas and practical styling guidance into one thoughtful space, helping you turn everyday inspiration into a look that feels like your own.

Explore outfit photographs and short videos across different ways of dressing. Discover relaxed layers, considered tailoring, streetwear combinations and subtle finishing touches. Visit editorial profiles, browse their visual collections and follow the styles you want to return to. Save favorite looks with a like and find them again in your personal collection.

Give your next outfit a clear direction. Choose from eight visual styles: Minimalist, Streetwear, Vintage, Soft Tailoring, Smart Casual, Cozy, City Chic and Weekend. Add a reference photo to your inspiration board and select an occasion, from a working day or a weekend away to dinner and travel.

Ask for text-based guidance on colors, proportions, shoes, accessories and layering. Describe the pieces you already own, explore an alternative combination or refine the finishing detail. On supported devices, the stylist can use on-device AI. Curated style guidance is available when that model is unavailable. Reference photos help you organize your ideas; they are not analyzed by the stylist.

Create a personal style space with your own profile, photos and wardrobe entries. Keep outfit notes, save drafts and write comments alongside the looks that interest you. Explore themed rooms with curated content and decorative reactions. Editorial characters, room activity and viewer counts are presentation content rather than verified live broadcasts or real-time audience activity.

Make the experience your own with collectible gifts, profile charms and an original avatar frame. Visit the daily check-in, track your coin balance and review your activity. The first five stylist replies are free. Additional completed replies use 20 coins each, and optional coin packs are available through Apple In-App Purchase.

Control what appears in your collection. Report content to save a local report and hide it, or block a profile to remove its content from your view. Likes, comments, follows, wardrobe entries, styling history and local wallet progress are saved on your device. Cross-device synchronization, private-message delivery and connected voice or video calls are not currently available. An internet connection is needed to load the catalog, photos and videos.

From the first idea to the final accessory, Tutu helps you pay attention to the choices that make a look yours.
